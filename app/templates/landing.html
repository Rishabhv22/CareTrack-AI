The Chronic Care Gap

The Consultation Blind Spot

Once a chronic disease patient leaves the clinic, the doctor loses all visibility into their daily health metrics, dietary choices, exercise compliance, and medication adherence.

Patients return months later for follow-ups with incomplete or forgotten logs, forcing doctors to make complex medical decisions based on spot checkups rather than longitudinal data.

CareTrack AI Solution

Continuous Insights & Trends

CareTrack AI bridges the gap. Patients complete a quick daily health check-in, write symptom diaries, and upload medical reports.

Using OCR lab result parsing, NLP diary summary, and Machine Learning trend grouping, CareTrack AI aggregates this information into an actionable dashboard for the clinician's review.

How CareTrack AI Works

1. Doctor Prescribes

Doctor diagnoses patient and adds target conditions (Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension) and prescribed medications.

2. Patient Check-In

Patient logs blood pressure, glucose, weight, active minutes, and uploads standard lab test files daily or weekly.

3. AI & ML Process

The engine extracts lab values, runs NLP diary keyword extraction, compiles adherence scores, and clusters trends.

4. Doctor Reviews

Doctor evaluates compliance statistics and progress flags on their dashboard before adjusting care plans.

CareTrack AI Key Features

Advanced capabilities structured for future healthcare clinics.

Lab Report OCR Parser

Automatically parse standard lab values (HbA1c, Glucose, Lipids) from uploaded PDF or image files. Correct reading errors with a review workflow before saving.

Daily Health Logging

Patients track biometric markers (Blood pressure, glucose) and compliance checkboxes (meds, diet, exercise) in under a minute.

NLP Journal Analyzer

Identify specific symptoms (fatigue, pain, dizziness) and compliance concerns from free-text diaries using natural language processing.

Care Adherence Score

Calculate an transparent scoring index (0-100) based on medication logging, exercise consistency, diet check-ins, and clinic follow-ups.

Machine Learning Trends

Train Random Forest & Logistic Regression classification models to predict patient progress trends and cluster patient compliance groupings.

Clinician Alert System

Flags requiring clinician review (e.g. low compliance thresholds, critical biometric fluctuations) are automatically pushed to the doctor dashboard.

Clinical Safety Declaration

CareTrack AI is a clinical support assistant and patient trend analysis tool. It is NOT an AI doctor, does not perform medical diagnosis, and does not prescribe or change drug dosages. The attending physician is the sole authorized decision-maker for all medical treatments.
